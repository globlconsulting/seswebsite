SELLEBRITY 2.0 COMING SOON

The definitive playbook for attracting, serving, and keeping high-profile clients.

To build a world-class practice servicing professional athletes, entertainers, and prominent public figures, standard professional advice doesn't cut it. These clients operate in highly volatile environments, demand absolute discretion, and face complex legal, financial, and promotional realities.

Following the industry success of Sellebrity in 2017, Sellebrity 2.0 will be released in 2026 and takes Sports and Entertainment Professional training to another level. Sellebrity 2.0 is set to be the industry-leading certification textbook for this generation of SE professionals, serving as the official curriculum for the Certified Sports & Entertainment Professional (CSEP) designation. This book lays out the exact frameworks, compliance protocols, and strategic partnership tactics needed to elevate your service standards to the Sellebrity standard.
